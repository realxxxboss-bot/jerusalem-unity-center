Upcoming Events

The Jerusalem Unity Center gathers people from every nation for evenings of learning, dialogue, music, and reflection. Our events are open to all — no affiliation, background, or prior study required. Each one is an invitation to sit together, to listen well, and to leave with something worth carrying home.

Below you will find our next gathering: where it takes place, who is leading it, and what the evening will hold. Places are limited and offered on a first-come basis, so we encourage you to reserve early.

Support Our Mission

The Seventy Nations (R.A.) Jerusalem is a registered Israeli nonprofit association that partners with its sister organization, The Jerusalem Unity Center USA — a 501(c) nonprofit foundation that allows its donors to take tax deductions for contributions of cash, goods, and other assets.

Your support and contributions will enable us to meet our goals and improve conditions. Your generous donation will fund our mission.

  • Education Courses, pathways and scholarships open to every nation.
  • The Institutes Research and teaching in consciousness, ethics and dialogue.
  • Gatherings Cultural evenings, dialogues and international convenings.
  • The Campus Building the Center’s permanent home in Jerusalem.

The Vision

A center for welcoming the nations in Jerusalem — a global gathering place for learning, dialogue, spiritual development, cultural exchange, and human flourishing.

The Center holds a question that belongs to all people: what allows a human being, and a whole society, to flourish?

“The light within illuminates the path for all.” Jerusalem Unity Center
02

The Campus

A single continuous landscape rather than separate buildings — terraced pavilions stepping down the hillside above the city, gathered around gardens, courtyards, and walking paths.
